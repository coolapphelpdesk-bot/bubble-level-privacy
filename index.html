Privacy Policy

Bubble Level & Angle Meter · last updated November 10, 2026

1. Summary

Bubble Level & Angle Meter (“the app”) is a bubble level, clinometer and slope tool published by TeamCoolApps.

  • Sensor readings are measured and shown entirely on your device. They are not stored, logged or uploaded.
  • Camera frames are shown live only. A photo is saved to your device only when you tap Take a photo, and it is never sent anywhere.
  • The app contains Google’s advertising SDK (AdMob) for one banner on the Tools list, never on a measuring screen. Ads are switched off until September 1, 2027: until then the SDK is never started, makes no network requests and collects nothing. From that date it may collect the device data listed in section 2. There are no analytics or crash-reporting SDKs.
  • There is no account and no sign-in. Settings and calibration offsets stay in the app’s private storage on your device.
  • The only thing that ever reaches the developer is the feedback message you choose to send, together with the app version and device model described in section 5. Your measurements, camera view and photos never reach the developer or Google.

2. Data we collect

The app itself collects no personal data. It does not collect your name, email address, phone number, contacts, location, photos, files or usage statistics.

The one exception from the app is the optional feedback form: if you write a message and tap Send, that message is delivered to the developer’s feedback endpoint. Sending feedback is entirely voluntary, and you can use every measuring feature without ever doing it.

The Google AdMob SDK included in the app is inactive until September 1, 2027; before that date it is never initialised and nothing below is collected. From that date, when the Tools list shows a banner, AdMob may collect:

AdMob never receives your measurements, calibration, camera view or photos: the app gives the ad SDK nothing from them. Google processes this data under the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

5. Network activity and third parties

The app has no server of its own and no account system. Apart from the ad banner described below, its only network request is the feedback form:

What is sentWhy
The message you write, the feedback type and the subjectTo read and answer your feedback
Your email address, only if you type one in the optional contact fieldSo the developer can reply to you
App version and version code, device manufacturer and model, Android version and API level, app locale, timestampTo reproduce bugs and understand which device the report is about

This information is sent over HTTPS to a feedback collector operated for TeamCoolApps apps. It is used only to handle your message. It is not sold, not used for advertising, and not combined with any other data.

Third-party SDKs:

  • Google AdMob — included, but inactive until September 1, 2027: the SDK is not initialised before that date, so it makes no requests and reads no identifiers. From that date it loads one banner on the Tools list (never on the Level, Angle, Camera or calibration screens) and may collect the device data in section 2. Where required (for example in the EEA and UK), you will be asked for consent before personalised ads are shown.

The app contains no analytics SDK, no crash-reporting SDK and no social SDK. No third party receives your measuring data, camera view or saved photos.

6. Children’s privacy

The app is a general-purpose measuring tool intended for general audiences and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The feedback form is the only way to contact the developer and it requires a deliberate action by the user.

7. Data retention and deletion

Nothing is stored on a server except feedback you choose to send. Feedback messages are kept only as long as needed to handle them.

  • To delete all local settings and calibration data: Android Settings → Apps → Bubble Level & Angle Meter → Storage → Clear storage, or uninstall the app.
  • To have a feedback message and any contact address deleted, email coolapphelpdesk@gmail.com with the approximate date you sent it.

Data collected by Google AdMob (from September 1, 2027) is retained by Google under its own policies. You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Google → Ads (or Settings → Privacy → Ads, depending on your device).

8. Security

Local data is protected by Android’s app sandbox: other apps cannot read the app’s private settings without root access. Feedback and ad traffic are transmitted over HTTPS. No method of transmission over the internet is completely secure, but the app keeps the amount of data it sends to the minimum needed to answer your message.

9. Changes to this policy

If the app’s data behaviour changes, this page will be updated and the date at the top will change. Material changes will be noted in the app’s release notes on Google Play. Continuing to use the app after an update means you accept the revised policy.
